Bipolar Express is marathon stage design production on the high heels in the desert. Very intensive project schedule in the harsh environment of one of the most memorable festivals in the world.

Two train shape art cars function as left and right sides of mobile stage, packed with light fixtures, lasers and Function. All programmed with Touch Designer and linked to remote iPad control with custom control layout.  One rig we did one of the brightest and loudest appearances among new participants of the festival in 2024.
