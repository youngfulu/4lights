Inside a Loom Factory, the concept reinterprets the Jacquard machine: a form that nods to both historic looms and contemporary industrial machinery. Jacquard, pattern, and sound are tied together—texture is processed into sound, musical waves map onto pattern on cloth. Punch cards and reels carry the logic of the weave into the space. The visual language can shift from bright acid green to space grey and gentle blue, with no fixed limit on colour. The result is a spatial and sonic translation of Missoni’s weaving heritage into an immersive, reactive environment.
