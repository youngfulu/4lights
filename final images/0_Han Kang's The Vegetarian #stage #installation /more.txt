Originally written by Han Kang, directed by Evgeniya Safonova, video by Alina Tikhonova, Germany 2025 
Han Kang’s Nobel Prize-winning novel provides the foundation for this radical exploration of feminine rebellion and physical autonomy. A masterpiece of contemporary literature and a manifesto of silent subversion, The Vegetarian is structured as a haunting psychological journey through three acts of transformation. The production premiered on September 19, 2025, in the Alte Chirurgie (Old Surgery), a historic lecture hall at the University of Heidelberg. 

The participants in this visceral drama are Yong-hye, who decides to stop eating meat after a series of bloody nightmares, and the societal structures that attempt to break her resistance. In the Heidelberg production, the narrative is intensified by Safonova’s signature use of macro-video projections and a central, clinical stage element—a sexiertisch (dissection table)—where the protagonist’s quiet revolt against human cruelty is laid bare.
