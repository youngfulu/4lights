Spatial and light design for Mirage Cinema on Bolshoy, Saint Petersburg. Light is the main tool: it guides movement, marks the transition from lobby to auditoriums, and ties the space to the history of cinema. The two-level lobby moves from silver to the venue’s signature red; light cubes shift mood for premieres and events; red-lit stairways echo darkroom and film heritage. Each hall has its own light and sound character, so the building reads as a sequence of distinct atmospheres rather than a neutral container.
