Public-space design concept for a central Moscow site: a corridor formed by two building facades and an inner courtyard that had remained out of use. On the initiative of Stereo Tactics agency and the project proposes revitalising this space as a multifunctional zone—bar, restaurant, public area, and a small concert venue. The design is informed by the narrow, high-density streets of Kowloon, Hong Kong, where built form forces maximum use of every space available; the concept translates that efficiency and intensity into a compact programme and spatial organisation.
