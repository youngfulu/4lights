Pathway (also known as Crossroads Stone) is a light and sound installation for 2GIS at the Signal festival in Nikola-Lenivets art park. The concept centres on wayfinding and choice: “Give me a sign of where to go.” A central monolith or rock in the landscape carries a projection—branded with 2GIS—while vertical light tubes mark the space around it, so the piece reads as both landmark and moment of decision. Visitors could “select scene” or “get a hint,” tying the installation to the festival map and to 2GIS’s role as a guide. The setting is the park at night: forest, terrain, and natural forms sit alongside the lit structure, so navigation and place feel like one idea rather than a layer on top of the site.
