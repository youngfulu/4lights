Stage design, technical support, and control engine for the club side of Mirage Cinema. 

The venue sits inside the reconceived cinema building on Petrograd Side: the club shares the same spatial and light language—lobby light cubes, silver-to-red gradient, and distinct atmospheres per zone. The stage and show control extend that idea into the live programme, so the club feels like one more “hall” in the building’s narrative of light and cinema.
