View Point is a light and spatial installation for 2GIS at Sevkabel Port in Saint Petersburg. The project turns the industrial waterfront into a visible landmark: light defines the space and guides the eye, so the installation works as both wayfinding and atmosphere. Conceived for outdoor use in the port environment, it holds its own in changing weather and at night—the emphasis is on clarity and presence in the landscape rather than decorative effect, aligning with 2GIS’s role as a guide to the city.
