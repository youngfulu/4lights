Based on Eve Ensler’s collection of monologues, this production serves as a powerful, high-tech exploration of the global female experience. A manifesto of contemporary documentary theatre, I am an Emotional Being is structured as a series of intimate, high-stakes testimonies that challenge societal constraints on the female body and soul.

The participants in this visceral drama are three performers whose presence is amplified by Alina Tikhonova’s immersive video design, which transforms the stage into a digital canvas of micro-emotions. In the Vaba Lava production, the narrative is intensified by a clinical, neon-lit aesthetic, creating a sharp dialogue between the raw vulnerability of the live voice and the cold, magnified precision of the video projections.
