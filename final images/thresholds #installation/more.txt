Thresholds explores liminality from several angles: as physical space, psychological state, and rite of passage. By warping the gap between physical and digital realms, the installation merges spaces inside the former Pieter Baan Centre, forming an in-between state of past and present. In this limbo the man-made structure is no longer a psychiatric clinic nor an art space, but a place of pure liminality—marked by duality and the intimate experience of being stuck between states, of being in constant transition.
