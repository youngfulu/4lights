Wish Circles is an interactive spatial installation created for Nuanu Creative City in Bali, Indonesia. It forms part of Aurora Park — Bali’s first multimedia outdoor park — a one-kilometer trail of light, sound, and interactive art along the riverbank in Nuanu’s jungle.

The piece consists of six interactive mirrors whose reflective surfaces react to visitors and drive polyrhythmic sound and light. As people move around and interact with the installation, the mirrors trigger shifting light patterns and rhythmic, layered audio, turning the circle into a shared, responsive environment. The result sits at the intersection of spatial design, sound, and participation: the space is activated by the presence and movement of the audience.

Wish Circles was produced by Delivered in collaboration with Nuanu, within a park of eight permanent, climate-resistant installations controlled by a single system. The project aligns with Nuanu’s aim to turn visitors from spectators into active participants, and with Aurora Park’s focus on combining art, nature, and technology in an immersive outdoor setting.
