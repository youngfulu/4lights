Collaboration with SETUP and VISION-FACTORY for Dom Dolla’s live shows at Red Rocks Amphitheatre (Denver) and Coachella (California). At Coachella 2024, Dom Dolla closed the Sahara Tent on Saturday with a headline set that included a surprise guest appearance by Nelly Furtado (mashups and their collaboration “Eat Your Man”), and on Friday he played a back-to-back with John Summit as Everything Always at the Outdoor Theatre. Video content, lasers, and lighting programming support both the arena-scale Red Rocks run and the festival sets—unified visual language across venues while each show keeps its own focus.
