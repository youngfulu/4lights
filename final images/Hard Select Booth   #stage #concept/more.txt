This concept focuses on creating decor with a strong industrial aesthetic. Objects are chosen to work both as striking artistic elements and as practical lighting—repurposing items from factory environments to build an authentic rave atmosphere.

The core of the stage is a modular, reusable metal structure with sleek, geometric forms. This large yet lightweight architecture is a versatile foundation for the overall stage design.

The alternative backdrop uses a multi-layered setup: an LED screen, an acrylic layer for subtle diffusion, and a fine metal mesh overlay that reinforces the industrial look.

Additional decorative elements include industrial fans with integrated lighting; engine cranes with lighting fixtures that draw the attention of guests in the front rows and can carry new compositions of lights or multimedia for each event (e.g. two units); and scissor lifters with integrated lighting that add an industrial feel and act as mobile rigging stations to illuminate the sides of the dance floor (e.g. six units).
