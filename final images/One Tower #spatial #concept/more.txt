Installation “Capsule”
The installation becomes a visualization of the gentle process of the birth of an idea. The abstract form of the capsule contains a concept that develops directly before the eyes of city dwellers. Spectators can observe the process of “maturing” and “growing” of a metaphysical idea into a physical building. (Artist options for partnership — work in progress.)

Fence
The fence consists of two layers. The outer layer is a perforated metal sheet or metal mesh: a wear-resistant, monolithic material that gives a minimal, clean look. The inner layer is lighting fixtures, so that any inscription or image can be displayed on the fence. The fence lighting is programmed in sync with the installation’s light design—fence and installation work as one.

A bionic fence in the shape of a Möbius strip is set on edge. The upper part is a light strip showing abstract graphics, visible to pedestrians. The strip is used only as a lighting element, not as a media facade.

Portal for pedestrians
A portal is also provided for pedestrians: an interactive photo zone is integrated into the fence. Passers-by can look into the world of the future in the same way as the inhabitants of the towers.
