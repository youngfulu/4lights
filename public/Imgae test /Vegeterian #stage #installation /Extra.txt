Bluebeard's Castle

Create video and stage design 
for Perm Opera Ballet Theatre 
Originally created by Bela Bartok, directed by Evgeniya Safonova, conducted Fedor Lednev, Russia 2022

Béla Bartók’s only opera made the Hungarian composer a classic of the musical theatre of the 20th century. The epitome of modernist operatic form and a manifesto of  expressionism, Bluebeard’s Castle is structured as a one-act thriller. Winners of the Golden Mask Award in the nomination for best work of a production designer ina musical theatre.

The participants in the psychological drama are the duke, his young wife Judith and the symphony orchestra, which from the first to the last note carries on a tense dialogue with the two singers on stage. In the Perm's opera Bluebeard’s Castle will be performed in the frame of the prologue and epilogue, written for Bartok’s score by composer Valeriy Voronov (Belarus / Germany).
